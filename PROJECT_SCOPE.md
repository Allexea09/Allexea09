# International STEM Program
## Project Scope Statement

### 1. Project Overview
The International STEM Program is designed to establish and sustain educational opportunities in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics through strategic partnerships, local sponsorships, community engagement, and structured program management.

### 2. Project Objectives
- Establish a sustainable STEM education initiative.
- Coordinate local sponsorships and strategic partnerships.
- Manage the $1.5 million secured funding plan over a three-year sustainability period.
- Develop program resources, implementation schedules, and operational procedures.
- Establish communication, reporting, and accountability frameworks.
- Prepare for the official ribbon-cutting ceremony scheduled for September 12, 2025.

### 3. Scope of Work
- Project initiation, planning, governance, and documentation.
- Agile/Hybrid sprint planning and milestone tracking.
- Budget administration and administrative cost analysis.
- Sponsor, donor, and community partnership coordination.
- STEM program development and educational resource planning.
- Stakeholder communications and donation campaign coordination.
- Risk management, quality reviews, and progress reporting.
- Operational readiness and launch event coordination.

### 4. Key Deliverables
- Project charter and scope statement.
- Project schedule and sprint roadmap.
- Sponsorship and partnership communication strategy.
- Budget oversight and administrative cost analysis framework.
- Program implementation and operational readiness plans.
- Risk register, status reports, and milestone documentation.
- Ribbon-cutting and launch coordination plan.

### 5. Project Timeline
- **Project Start:** May 5, 2024
- **Core Implementation:** May 2024 – April 2025
- **Closeout and Readiness:** May – July 2025
- **Launch Preparation:** August 2025
- **Ribbon Cutting:** September 12, 2025

### 6. Project Resources
- Secured local sponsorship funding totaling $1.5 million.
- Community partners and sponsors.
- STEM educators and program stakeholders.
- Project coordination, communications, and administrative resources.

### 7. Out of Scope
Activities not approved in the project charter, unbudgeted expenditures, and commitments beyond the agreed program objectives require separate review and authorization.

### 8. Success Criteria
- Completion and approval of required project deliverables.
- Documented sponsorship and partnership commitments.
- Transparent financial tracking and administrative cost analysis.
- Verified program readiness and stakeholder coordination.
- Documented launch approvals and event readiness.

### 9. Governance and Change Control
Project scope, schedule, budget, and deliverable changes must be documented, assessed for impact, and approved by the authorized project stakeholders.

**Documentation Note:** This scope statement describes the intended project framework. Actual completion, approvals, expenditures, and outcomes should be supported by project records.
