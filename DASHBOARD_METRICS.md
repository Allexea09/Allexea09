## 4. Project Milestones & Schedule Performance

Project milestone tracking supports accountability for planning, implementation, and delivery.

| Milestone Area | Deliverable or Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Program Planning | Approved program objectives and implementation plan | Update with verified progress |
| Financial Planning | Approved budget and funding documentation | Update with verified progress |
| Learning Resources | STEM curriculum, educational materials, and equipment plan | Update with verified progress |
| Facility Readiness | Documented readiness of the STEM learning environment, if applicable | Update with verified progress |
| Training Delivery | Student and teacher training records | Update with verified progress |
| Partnerships | Documented school and community agreements | Update with verified progress |
| Program Evaluation | KPI reports and participant feedback | Update with verified progress |
| Sustainability Planning | Three-year operating and funding plan | Update with verified progress |

### Schedule KPIs

- **Milestone Completion Rate:** Completed milestones divided by planned milestones × 100.
- **On-Time Delivery Rate:** Milestones completed on or before their approved due dates divided by milestones due × 100.
- **Schedule Variance:** Actual completion date compared with the approved baseline date.
- **Open Action Items:** Outstanding tasks requiring ownership or resolution.

Record the approved baseline, actual completion date, accountable owner, and supporting evidence for each milestone. Do not mark milestones complete until supporting records are available.

## 5. Three-Year Sustainability Framework

### Year 1 — Establish and Implement

- Confirm program governance, operational responsibilities, and funding requirements.
- Establish approved budgets, performance baselines, and reporting procedures.
- Develop educational resources and implementation partnerships.
- Begin or expand program delivery according to the approved schedule.

### Year 2 — Measure and Improve

- Review participation, training, feedback, and delivery performance.
- Assess spending against the approved budget.
- Strengthen effective school and community partnerships.
- Adjust program delivery based on documented outcomes and available resources.

### Year 3 — Evaluate and Sustain

- Evaluate progress against the original objectives and established baselines.
- Assess ongoing operating costs and future funding needs.
- Document program outcomes, lessons learned, and improvement opportunities.
- Develop a continuation plan based on verified results and available resources.

**Planning Note:** These are proposed planning phases. Actual completion dates and results should be documented as implementation proceeds.

## 8. Data Quality & Accountability

To maintain reliable reporting:

- Use source documents, attendance records, training logs, invoices, agreements, and approved project schedules.
- Identify every metric as a target, actual result, estimate, or pending value.
- Assign an owner and reporting period to each KPI.
- Review calculations before publishing results.
- Protect student and participant privacy; publish aggregate data rather than personally identifiable information.
- Keep financial and program records consistent with applicable funding agreements and reporting requirements.
