### Monitoring and Controlling — Key Control Activities

#### 1. Control Changes to Scope, Schedule, and Cost

**Objective:** Ensure changes to project scope, schedule, and budget are evaluated, documented, and approved before implementation.

**Activities:**
- Compare project deliverables against the approved scope baseline.
- Monitor milestones, dependencies, deadlines, and schedule variances.
- Compare actual expenditures and forecasts against the approved cost baseline.
- Document change requests, including the reason, business need, and expected benefit.
- Assess each proposed change for its impact on scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources, risks, and program objectives.
- Obtain approval from the authorized project sponsor or decision-maker before implementing changes that affect approved baselines.
- Update project plans, budgets, schedules, risk registers, and stakeholder communications after approval.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

| KPI | Measurement | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Scope Change Compliance | Approved scope changes / total implemented scope changes × 100 | 100% |
| Schedule Variance | Actual schedule performance compared with the approved baseline | Within approved tolerance |
| Cost Variance | Budgeted cost of work performed minus actual cost, using earned value management where applicable | Within approved tolerance |
| Change Request Tracking | Number of open, approved, rejected, and completed change requests | Updated monthly |

**Expected Outcome:** Controlled changes, documented decisions, improved budget discipline, and alignment with approved project objectives.

---

#### 2. Conduct Quality Control

**Objective:** Verify that project deliverables, educational resources, and program activities meet established requirements and acceptance criteria.

**Activities:**
- Establish quality standards and acceptance criteria for major deliverables.
- Review STEM educational materials, workshop plans, training resources, and technology requirements.
- Inspect deliverables for completeness, accuracy, usability, and alignment with program objectives.
- Validate program data, financial records, and KPI calculations before reporting.
- Document quality issues, defects, corrective actions, and resolution dates.
- Collect feedback from educators, students, families, and community partners where appropriate.
- Verify that corrective actions resolve identified quality issues.
- Maintain quality review records to support accountability and continuous improvement.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

| KPI | Measurement | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Deliverable Acceptance Rate | Deliverables accepted / deliverables reviewed × 100 | To be established |
| Quality Issue Resolution | Quality issues resolved / quality issues due for resolution × 100 | To be established |
| Data Accuracy | Verified records meeting established accuracy standards | To be established |
| Corrective Action Closure | Corrective actions completed by their due dates | To be established |

**Expected Outcome:** Reliable deliverables, accurate reporting, consistent program standards, and continuous improvement.

---

#### 3. Report Status to Stakeholders

**Objective:** Provide timely, accurate, and transparent updates on project performance, risks, financial status, milestones, and program outcomes.
