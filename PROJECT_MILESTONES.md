# Project Milestone Roadmap & Sprint Completion Plan
**Project Start:** May 5, 2024  
**Core Implementation:** May 2024 – April 2025  
**Project Closeout:** May – July 2025  
**Final Launch & Ribbon Cutting:** September 12, 2025

## Project Objectives
- Establish project governance, scope, deliverables, and implementation schedule.
- Coordinate STEM program development, community partnerships, and sponsorships.
- Manage the $1.5 million locally sponsored funding plan and administrative cost analysis.
- Track sprint deliverables, risks, dependencies, and milestone completion.
- Prepare the program, site, stakeholders, and communications for the ribbon-cutting ceremony.

## Milestone Gates

- **Gate 1 — Project Initiation:** Charter, scope, governance, and stakeholders established.
- **Gate 2 — Funding & Partnerships:** Sponsorship coordination, budget controls, and partner engagement reviewed.
- **Gate 3 — Program Design:** Program requirements, curriculum, and resources reviewed.
- **Gate 4 — Implementation Review:** Core deliverables and risks assessed.
- **Gate 5 — Operational Readiness:** Outstanding actions, approvals, and logistics validated.
- **Gate 6 — Official Launch:** Ribbon-cutting ceremony scheduled for September 12, 2025.

## Project Controls

- **Methodology:** Agile / Hybrid
- **Sprint Cadence:** Approximately two weeks
- **Reporting:** Sprint reviews, milestone reports, risk register, and action log
- **Financial Oversight:** Budget variance, administrative cost analysis, and expenditure tracking
- **Communication:** Sponsor updates, partner meetings, donor communications, and impact reporting
- **Change Control:** Document scope changes, dependencies, risks, and approval decisions.

## Completion Criteria

Each sprint should have documented deliverables, an assigned owner, acceptance criteria, and a review record. Milestones should be marked complete only after evidence and required approvals are recorded.
