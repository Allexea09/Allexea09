## 🎯 Executive Summary
This project was initiated, developed, and funded by DVHD to bridge the critical digital divide in rural Georgia. As the founder and principal architect, I directed all vendor negotiations, designed the academic frameworks, managed stakeholder relationships with regional club directors, and maintained full fiduciary responsibility for program execution.

## 🎯 Strategic Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)
* **Objective:** Establish a repeatable, scalable STEM framework capable of running independently of highly specialized tech infrastructure.
  * **KR 1:** Train and certify 5 local non-technical facilitators within 6 weeks.
  * **KR 2:** Onboard 100+ active weekly participants across core modules (Scratch Coding, Basic Mechanics).
  * **KR 3:** Secure 100% data reporting compliance required for federal and private grant metrics.
